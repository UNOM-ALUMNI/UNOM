Our Legacy

Hall of Fame & Notable Achievers

Honoring legendary minds, scientists, and leaders shaped by our institution.

Sir C.V. Raman

Physics & Science

Achievement: Discovered the Raman Effect regarding the scattering of light.

Accolade: Recipient of the Nobel Prize in Physics (1930), the first Asian to win a Nobel Prize in science.

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Aerospace & Leadership

Achievement: Led India's missile development and civilian space program breakthroughs.

Accolade: Served as the 11th President of India; awarded the Bharat Ratna.

Srinivasa Ramanujan

Mathematics

Achievement: Prolific contributions to mathematical analysis, number theory, and infinite series.

Accolade: Elected as a Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS) in London.

Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar

Astrophysics

Achievement: Calculated the mass limit of white dwarf stars (Chandrasekhar Limit).

Accolade: Awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics (1983).

M.S. Swaminathan

Agricultural Science

Achievement: Principal architect of India's Green Revolution, preventing massive food crises.

Accolade: Recipient of the Padma Vibhushan and World Food Prize.

Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan

Philosophy & Governance

Achievement: Prominent philosopher-statesman who bridged Eastern and Western thought.

Accolade: Served as the 2nd President of India; birthday celebrated as Teachers' Day.
